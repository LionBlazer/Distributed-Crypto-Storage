# Exact nonnegative-prefix criterion

These are implementation-specific algebraic derivations for the research cost
estimator. They establish coefficient signs, not lattice hardness, Fiat–Shamir
security or an accepting DCS proof. All parameters here are exact integers.

Let H(z)=prod(1-z^d)^m/(1-z)^n, n>0, d>0, m>=0, with precision L>0. Only
coefficients0 through L-1 belong to the original estimator's search. Removing
zero exponents and factors with d>=L preserves that entire prefix; equal degrees
combine exponents. If the sum of the remaining exponents M is at most n, then

```text
H(z) = prod((1+z+...+z^(d-1))^m) * (1-z)^(-(n-M))  (mod z^L).
```

Every factor has nonnegative coefficients. Zeros remain possible, but no strictly
negative coefficient exists in the requested prefix. This preserves the exact
original infinity Cost, including its fields/order/metadata. It does not replace
a timeout, error or failed proof with infinity.

For two remaining distinct degrees suppose one exponent is exactly n:

```text
H(z) = (1-z^d)^m * q(z),  q(z)=(1+z+...+z^(s-1))^n.
K=L-1, B=n+K-1, r=K/B, D[k]=binomial(n+k-1,k), D[k]=0 for k<0.
```

The L=1 case is already covered above. Otherwise B>0 and0<=r<=1. D[k] counts
compositions of k into n nonnegative parts. q[k] counts those whose parts are
all below s, so a union bound over the n forbidden coordinates gives
`D[k]-n*D[k-s] <= q[k] <= D[k]` for k>=0. For a<=k<=K,

```text
D[k-a]/D[k] = prod((k-i)/(n+k-1-i), i=0..a-1) <= r^a.
```

For a>k the left side is zero. Consequently q[k]>=D[k]*(1-n*r^s), while the
absolute contribution from all nonconstant terms of (1-z^d)^m is at most

```text
sum(binomial(m,j)*q[k-j*d], j>=1)
  <= D[k]*sum((m*r^d)^j, j>=1)
  = D[k]*x/(1-x),  x=m*r^d<1.
```

Thus `n*r^s<=1/2` and `x<=1/4` imply H[k]>=D[k]/6>0 for every0<=k<=K.
The implementation tests these with integer inequalities, without floating-point
rounding. It tests e=min(d,80): `2*n*K^s<=B^s` and `4*m*K^e<=B^e`.
Since r<=1 and e<=d, m*r^d<=m*r^e; this is a conservative sufficient criterion,
not truncation of H or a changed precision. If either test fails, all coefficients
are generated by the exact sparse recurrence with the original stopping rule.

Independent finite-binomial tests cover accepted positive cases and actual
negative cases. In particular n4096 with a single degree41 exponent2^31+4096
still has its first negative at6615, verified against independent binomial
coefficients on both sides of that boundary. Sage oracle and full estimator
results are separately reported in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md).
