## Candidate boundary

coefficients to the successful lock walk's returned original bytes. OFFSET-owned
but unreturned rows do not become returned data. Canonical and certified consumers
share this new AIR/Kind but bind distinct exact producer contexts and statement
cases. The distinct [BfvLockReturnedFormation](src/opening/locking/formation/README.md) relation binds the same returned
encoding to shared KeyTransform, five Basis proofs and the existing bounded local
integer-formation kernel. Old SelectedEncoding/LocalFormation proofs are not
substitutable by renaming their roots or Kind.

The certified cold-plus-two-warm formation scenario passed exactly once inside
the full suite. It has no separate CLI or per-query timing JSON. Only seven new
README status sections and this report change after validation; the five published
